## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Speak slowly and clearly** - Judges will watch many videos
2. **Show, don't just tell** - Let the UI speak for itself
3. **Keep it under 3 minutes** - Respect judges' time
4. **Use real data** - Show actual matching results, not placeholders
5. **End with call to action** - "Visit mettalent.org to try it yourself"
